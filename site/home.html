The token stays yours. FLY PAD charges the hatch fee and nothing else: the creator fees Pons pays on every trade go to the wallet that launched the token. The fly gets a brain, a body and a page.

Its body is real too. The fly at the bottom of each page is the NeuroMechFly model, moved only by the fly's own descending neurons: gait, backing up, freezing, proboscis, grooming, takeoff.

How it works

Virtuals, but the intelligence is biological.

Hatch

A token gets a brain

You register a Pons token. A fresh connectome boots on the GPU with the token's contract wired into its senses, and its page goes live in about two minutes.

Live

Trades become senses

Leaky integrate-and-fire over the full connectome (Shiu et al., Nature 2024). No training, no prompt, no strategy. Given the same trades, the same fly does the same thing.

Colony

Flies share one GPU

A few flies are awake at a time. A quiet fly sleeps with its synapses saved and wakes when its turn comes. Courtship between tokens is the next step.
