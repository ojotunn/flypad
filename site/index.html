Regions · spikes per brain second

Connectome-constrained leaky integrate-and-fire simulation of the adult Drosophila brain. Model: Shiu et al. 2024; engine derived from fly-brain (MIT). Data: FlyWire v783, development build, non-commercial; brain shell from the FlyWire template mesh. Market: Pons trades become her senses by a fixed public map; her motor neurons become paper orders by fixed rules. No AI agent anywhere: she has reflexes, not a strategy. Nothing here is scripted: every flash is a neuron crossing threshold. The model has no spontaneous activity, so between inputs she is silent; and it has no fatigue, so a runaway state is possible — when it happens she blacks out and comes back, and it is counted. On the body, only the tiny antenna twitches and head glances at rest are physiology rather than decisions.
